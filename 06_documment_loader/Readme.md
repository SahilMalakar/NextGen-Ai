**Toxic Love**


**GROQ_Respose**
In a garden where the roses bleed,
Petals drip with thorns of spite,
We drink a wine that’s half‑sweet, half‑need,
And stumble through the endless night.

Your eyes, a glass of storm‑lit glass,
Reflect the fire that burns us both,
A whispered promise, fragile as ash,
Turns to ash when we breathe the oath.

We chase each other’s shadows,
A dance of broken mirrors,
Each kiss a jagged, hollow echo—
A lullaby for trembling liars.

The rope that ties us tight with gold,
Is frayed, it snaps, it pulls us down,
We’re bound by love that won’t let go,
A bitter tide that drowns the crown.

Yet in the wreckage, we still cling,
To the sweet poison of the ache,
Because even wrecked hearts still sing,
Of love that’s meant to bruise, not break.


**GEMINI_RESPONSE**

A velvet trap, a whisper soft and low,
Your honeyed words, a silver, binding chain.
I drank the nectar, not seeing the slow,
Insidious poison seeping through each vein.
A dazzling flame, too close for human touch,
You drew me in, desiring you too much.

We break and mend, a cycle old and grim,
The storm subsides, then gathers on the brim.
You offer solace, after the cruel sting,
Then tear the very peace you claim to bring.
My vibrant colors bled to shades of gray,
As your dark shadows chased my light away.

I search for me, in fragments of your gaze,
Lost in the labyrinth of your twisting maze.
Though every kiss a splinter in my soul,
I crave the wound that makes me feel whole.
A bittersweet delirium, hard to flee,
This beautiful destruction that is "we."

This gilded cage, where love pretends to bloom,
Has trapped my spirit in a shadowed room.
I bear the scars, where passion once did burn,
A lesson learned, though difficult to turn.
For toxic love, a cruel and artful art,
Can break a life, before it breaks a heart.
